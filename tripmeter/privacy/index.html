Roadtulip Trip Meter privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026. Roadtulip, published on Google Play by DEMETIO.

Roadtulip Trip Meter measures distance with your phone's GPS while you drive. It does not have an account system, does not show ads and does not send anything you do in the app to us or to anyone else.

Location

The app asks for precise location only when you tap Start. While a trip is running, the phone reads GPS positions so the app can add up distance, and Android shows a notification for as long as tracking is on. Tapping Stop ends tracking. The app never asks for location in the background when no trip is running.

Positions are used on the phone to calculate distance and are never uploaded or shared.

What is stored on the phone

Your settings (units, calibration factor) and the current counter values are saved on the phone so they survive a restart. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. They are not included in Android cloud backup.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro purchase is handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy; the app only receives a confirmation that Pro was bought, so it can unlock it. We do not see your payment details.

Children

The app is a driving tool and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date. Questions: use the developer email shown on the app's Google Play page.
